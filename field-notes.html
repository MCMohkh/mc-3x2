Field Notes№ 01
Creativity is not the deliverable.
What it makes possible is.
MCIdeas worth keeping

The opening statement

A logo, a film, a record or a campaign is only the thing you can hand over. What matters is what it allows: an audience, a career, a decision, a reason to come back. Creative work should serve a purpose beyond looking good.

Field Notes№ 02
Premium is an adjective. Quality is evidence.
MCIdeas worth keeping

Claiming value is not delivering it

Better materials, a considered experience, consistency, service and detail do more to establish quality than the word ever will. Show the evidence and the adjective becomes unnecessary.

The Fine Print№ 03
Attention is not the same as demand.
MCIdeas worth keeping

Visibility is a starting point

A campaign can get noticed without giving anyone a reason to buy, enquire, attend or return. Visibility is where the work begins, not the business outcome it was meant to produce.

The Fine Print№ 04
A million views can still leave you with nothing.
MCIdeas worth keeping

Measure what the work was for

Views, reach and impressions describe how many people passed by. They can't say who stayed, who enquired or what changed afterwards. Decide the outcome first, then count that.

Digital Shadow№ 05
Your Instagram is not your digital identity.
MCIdeas worth keeping

Identity is also what others say about you

Your public identity is shaped by search results, credits, interviews, project pages, metadata and credible independent references. Instagram is one room in that house, and you don't own the building.

Digital Shadow№ 06
If you can't be found, you can't be trusted.
MCIdeas worth keeping

Strangers check before they believe

A credible IMDb page, a Wikipedia entry and a Google Knowledge Panel are how a stranger decides you're real. The signal is quiet, and so is its absence.

People Are the IP№ 07
Being talented and being discoverable are two different problems.
MCIdeas worth keeping

Good work is necessary, not sufficient

Good work matters. So do positioning, credits, access, relationships and the ability to find the right person at the right time. Talent is one problem. Discoverability is another, and it needs its own answer.

People Are the IP№ 08
A talent roster is a list. A career is a body of work.
MCIdeas worth keeping

Representation is a long game

A list of names tells you who is on the books. A body of work tells you why they matter: the credits, the collaborators and the decisions that accumulate around one person.

Field Notes№ 09
A film's release is a date. Its audience is a relationship.
MCIdeas worth keeping

Cinema doesn't end on opening weekend

Opening-weekend attention is one moment. Discovery, cultural conversation, repeat viewing and long-term recall can extend a film's life, and the music, the campaign and the story around it all feed that.

Field Notes№ 10
Uploading music is distribution. Building a catalogue is a strategy.
MCIdeas worth keeping

A release can be delivered. A catalogue has to be managed

Delivery to streaming platforms is the easy part. Metadata, rights information, release planning, discoverability and long-term catalogue management decide whether a song is still findable in five years.

Field Notes№ 11
Talent gets you noticed. Reputation gets you hired.
MCIdeas worth keeping

Skill opens the door once

What people say when you're not in the room decides whether the door opens again.

Field Notes№ 12
A film is a thousand decisions someone had to defend.
MCIdeas worth keeping

Taste is made of stubborn choices

The same goes for a record, a brand and a campaign. You can see who was willing to argue for the detail.

Before the creative, the question is what the work stands for and who it is for. Behind the Work opens real projects: the challenge, the thinking behind the approach and what the work involved.
