Branding & Design

Brand strategy, identity, key art and digital design for artists, films, events and businesses — made by the team that also markets and distributes your work.

What We Do

From first research to final rollout

Brand strategy & research

  • Market, competitor and audience research
  • Brand purpose, mission, vision and core values
  • A clear, distinctive market position

Visual identity

  • Logos, wordmarks and brand symbols
  • Colour palettes, typography systems and icon sets
  • Brand guidelines: a rulebook for using logos, fonts and colours

Verbal identity & messaging

  • Names and taglines for companies, products and features
  • Brand voice, tone and editorial standards
  • Key messaging pillars and value propositions

Digital & web design

  • UI/UX concepts for websites and mobile apps
  • Your identity adapted for social media, email templates and digital assets
  • Responsive layouts, interfaces and motion that carry the brand onto the screen

Print & packaging

  • Product boxes, labels and retail packaging
  • Brochures, flyers, posters and annual reports
  • Banners, trade-show displays and large-format signage
  • Guidelines for merchandise and supporting collateral

Environmental graphics & signage

  • Wayfinding and signage systems for venues, exhibitions and events
  • Large-format graphics designed to work with the space they sit in

Campaigns, key art & editorial

  • Thematic and seasonal campaign identities across social, outdoor and merchandise
  • Film and series key art, posters, first looks and music artwork
  • Editorial design for magazines, brand journals and books

Brand rollout & activation

  • Launch campaigns and team alignment programmes
  • A consistent look and feel at every touchpoint: online, in store and in print

Not sure where to start? Tell us what you are launching and we will scope the right mix.
