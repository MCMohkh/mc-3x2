What We Work On

The Four Pillars of Notability.

Perception Management

How are you currently perceived — and how should you be? We help you understand the gap between your current narrative and the one that serves your goals, and develop a strategy to close it authentically.

Media Mentions & Coverage

Third-party media coverage is the single most powerful driver of notability. We identify the right publications, platforms and formats for your profile, and develop the angles that make your story worth covering.

Events & Public Appearances

Strategic presence at the right events builds credibility in ways that digital alone cannot. We advise on which engagements align with your positioning and how to maximise the value of public appearances.

Authority & Backlink Building

Credible external references — interviews, features, citations, industry profiles — create the web of authority that search engines and decision-makers both recognise. We build these systematically.

The Notability–Wikipedia Connection

PR Builds What Wikipedia Requires.

If you've been told you don't yet meet Wikipedia's notability guidelines — or if a Knowledge Panel hasn't generated for you yet — this is almost always a PR problem first. The solution isn't to force a Wikipedia article or claim a panel; it's to build the genuine notability that makes those things natural outcomes.

PR and notability first. Wikipedia and Knowledge Panel second.

Credible third-party media coverage in reliable sources is the foundation Wikipedia requires. It's also what signals to Google that you're worth generating a Knowledge Panel for. Building this foundation through honest, well-placed PR is the most durable and effective path.

We don't cut corners on this. We've seen what happens when profiles are built on weak sources or poor-quality coverage — they don't stick. What we build is designed to last.

Our Approach

  • Honest assessment of your current notability level and what it would take to build it genuinely
  • Identifying the media outlets, publications and platforms most relevant to your field
  • Developing story angles and pitches that are genuinely newsworthy — not promotional
  • Strategic timing of announcements, releases and coverage for maximum impact
  • Building a body of credible third-party references that support Wikipedia eligibility and Knowledge Panel generation
  • Long-term reputation planning aligned with your career or business trajectory
