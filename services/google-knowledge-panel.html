MC · Google Knowledge Panel

This Is Where Your Next Opportunity Begins Its Search.

When a producer, director, journalist or brand searches your name on Google, the Knowledge Panel is the first thing they see. It either builds instant trust — or raises questions. We help search engines understand exactly who you are, validate that identity with authoritative signals, and display it correctly.

Discuss Your Knowledge Panel
Why It Matters

Your Search Result Is a First Impression.

A Google Knowledge Panel is the information box that appears on the right side of search results when someone searches your name. It shows your photo, description, profession, notable works, social profiles and other key facts — pulled from across the web and structured by Google's Knowledge Graph.

Having one — and having it display accurate, complete information — signals to anyone searching for you that you are a recognised, verifiable public figure. Not having one, or having one with incorrect information, creates doubt exactly when you need trust.

"This is where your next potential hirer or audience is looking for you."

A strong presence on the search results page builds trust before a single conversation happens. Casting directors check. Brands check. Journalists check. Investors check. What they find shapes what happens next.

The Signals That Build a Panel

Google Doesn't Guess. It Reads Signals.

A Knowledge Panel is generated when Google has enough consistent, authoritative information about you from trusted sources. We help build and strengthen every one of these signals.

Wikipedia / Wikidata

The most authoritative signal Google reads. A well-sourced Wikipedia or Wikidata entry significantly accelerates panel generation.

Official Website

A website with correct structured data (Schema.org markup) helps Google understand your entity type and core facts.

IMDb Profile

For entertainment industry professionals, a complete and accurate IMDb profile is one of Google's most trusted data sources.

Social Media Profiles

Verified, consistent profiles across Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn and X contribute to Google's entity understanding.

Media Coverage

Third-party mentions in credible publications reinforce that you are a notable, real public figure worthy of a panel.

Consistency Across Sources

The same name, profession and facts appearing across multiple sources is what makes Google confident enough to display a panel.

What We Do

Our Knowledge Panel Service

We don't promise a Knowledge Panel — nobody can, because Google generates them algorithmically based on signals it deems sufficient. What we do is build those signals systematically and correctly, maximising the likelihood of panel generation and ensuring the information displayed is accurate.

  • Audit your current Google presence and identify what signals are missing or inconsistent
  • Build and optimise your Wikidata profile — a foundational structured data source for Google
  • Implement Schema.org structured data on your website for entity recognition
  • Ensure consistency of your name, profession and key facts across all platforms
  • Support the Knowledge Panel claim process once a panel is generated
  • Correct inaccurate or misleading information that appears in existing panels
