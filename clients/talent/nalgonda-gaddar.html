Nalgonda Gaddar Narsanna

A voice that belongs to the land. When Narsanna sings, it's not a performance — it's a people speaking through one throat. His songs played across rally grounds and village squares throughout both Telugu states during the 2023 elections, and the ground shook either way.

Get in touch with Nalgonda Gaddar Narsanna

About Narsanna

Nalgonda Gaddar Narsanna carries one of the most storied folk traditions of the Telugu-speaking world. His music is rooted in the Gaddar legacy — political, visceral, community-forged — yet distinctly his own. He doesn't just sing songs. He gives language to things people feel but can't articulate.

"In the 2023 Telugu state elections, Narsanna's songs rang out from the stages of every major party. Winning side, losing side — they all called for the same voice. That is not a small thing."

MC's relationship with Narsanna began in late 2021. We were introduced to him through a mutual connection in the Hyderabad cultural circuit, and we were immediately struck — not just by his voice, but by the weight he carries when he performs. He has an instinctive understanding of what a room needs, and the authority to deliver it.

The 2023 elections across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh confirmed what we had already suspected: Narsanna had become a force that transcended any single political or cultural affiliation. His songs were played at rallies for parties with opposing worldviews, because his music speaks to something more fundamental than positions or candidates. It speaks to Telugu identity itself.

Irrespective of who won or lost those elections, Nalgonda Gaddar Narsanna made a mark across both Telugu states that very few artists ever do. He is here to stay — for a very long time. We represent him for performance bookings, cultural commissions, brand collaborations, and any project that understands the power of music that actually means something.
