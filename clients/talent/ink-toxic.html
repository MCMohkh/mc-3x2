About INK Toxic

INK Toxic is an independent rapper and member of B12 Band, represented by MC. The name says something about the approach: his lyrics are deliberate, sharp, and leave a mark. He is one of the more technically focused rappers in the B12 collective — precise syllable counts, internal rhyme schemes, the kind of craft that reveals itself on second listen.

We identified INK Toxic through the same local cypher events where we found the rest of B12 Band. Each artist in the collective brings something the others don't, and INK Toxic brings a rigour with language that raises the level of every track he appears on.

Original material and full discography will be added to this page as his profile develops.

Credits

  • B12 Band
    Hip-Hop Collective · Hyderabad
    Core Member

INK Toxic brings a high-energy, street-influenced rap style to the B12 collective. His name references the idea that once his verses get into your head, they don't leave — and his delivery, all sharp edges and controlled aggression, makes that a credible claim. He works with MC on music distribution and artist development within Hyderabad's growing independent hip-hop scene.
