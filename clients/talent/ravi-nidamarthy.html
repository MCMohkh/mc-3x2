About Ravi Nidamarthy

Ravi Nidamarthy is a composer and singer whose music carries a sense of space and stillness that is rare in commercial Telugu cinema. Working primarily in the independent film ecosystem, he has built a quiet reputation as someone who scores films the way they deserve to be scored — from the inside out.

His work on Gopi Galla Goa Trip (2025, dir. Rohit Penumatsa) was singled out in virtually every review of the film. Critics described the score as 'one of the best Telugu albums in years' and praised its ability to enhance the film's coming-of-age road-trip mood without overwhelming it. His song 'Metal Marfa' — which he also sang — became a fan favourite.

He is also featured on Apple Music with his song 'Nallani Gopayya' and the single 'Kalala Kurisina' from Kanyakumari (2025). MC represents Ravi for talent bookings, film scoring projects, and live performances.

Before his film career, Ravi was a member of Godless — one of India's most acclaimed extreme metal exports. The Hyderabad-based death metal band toured Europe and Asia, performed at Wacken Open Air, and topped Bandcamp's global thrash charts with their 2021 album States of Chaos. That background in precision, intensity, and sonic architecture is audible in everything he scores today. He continues to work on music production across film, games, and other genres beyond Telugu cinema.
