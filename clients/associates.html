Mohammed Abrar

Fitness Entrepreneur · Dynevo & F45 Training Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad

Mohammed Abrar is the founder and owner of F45 Training Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad, operating under the Dynevo brand. He has built one of the city's most active and committed functional fitness communities — and taken it to the world stage.

His studio is Hyderabad's dominant force at HYROX — the global fitness racing event — having earned podium finishes and brought home flags from competitions in Bangkok, Bangalore, and Delhi.

With MC: we handle the studio's operations and team management, including onboarding and hiring, with marketing and social media support starting soon.
