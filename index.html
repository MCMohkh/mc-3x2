The Fine Print№ 01
Attention is not the same as demand.
MC JointIdeas worth keeping

Visibility is a starting point

A campaign can get noticed without giving anyone a reason to buy, enquire, attend or return. Visibility is where the work begins, not the business outcome it was meant to produce.

Digital Shadow№ 02
Your Instagram is not your digital identity.
MC JointIdeas worth keeping

Identity is also what others say about you

Your public identity is shaped by search results, credits, interviews, project pages, metadata and credible independent references. Instagram is one room in that house, and you don't own the building.

Field Notes№ 03
Premium is an adjective. Quality is evidence.
MC JointIdeas worth keeping

Claiming value is not delivering it

Better materials, a considered experience, consistency, service and detail do more to establish quality than the word ever will. Show the evidence and the adjective becomes unnecessary.
