Case Study

Pickpocket

Marketing a debut film made as a tribute to Robert Bresson.

ClientDivya Bandaru / Avanti Cinema
Year2022
MC roleMarketing partner
ServicesDigital strategy, distribution, soundtrack release

The film

Pickpocket is a Telugu film written and directed by Divya Bandaru. It follows Devi, an amateur pickpocket, and his friendship with Bhavani, a fellow small-time thief. It is Divya's debut and a tribute to Robert Bresson.

What we did

  • Planned the digital strategy and handled distribution as the film's marketing partner
  • Distributed the soundtrack, an 8-song album composed by Smaran, on Apple Music and other platforms

Where it stands

The full film and the official trailer are on YouTube, the soundtrack is on streaming platforms (watch Thodu Dongalu on YouTube), and the film is listed on IMDb. Posters, a first look and soundtrack artwork are collected on the project page. Divya is now working on The Murder Plan (Pickpocket 2), currently in post-production.

The project page has the full film, trailer, soundtrack links and complete credits.
