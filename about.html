About MC

More thanan agency.

Born in Hyderabad, built on passion. MC is where craft meets strategy, and vision meets execution.

MC was founded in 2018 with a single conviction: that great stories deserve great distribution.

We saw talented artists, filmmakers and creators in Hyderabad struggling not with their craft, but with reach, strategy and representation.

Starting with social media management and small music distribution deals, we grew into a full-service creative company: script development to brand sponsorships, talent auditions to digital strategy reports for state governments.

Today we work with composers like Vivek Sagar, cinema units like Avanti Cinema, India's first Muslim superhero comic Musalman Comix, and a growing roster of independent rap artists.

We don't position ourselves as vendors. We are partners, invested in your craft, your career and your community.

Sonu Mohkh, Founder

The team

The founders

Sonu Mohkh

Founder · Talent agent · Comic book writer

A seasoned marketing strategist with expertise in digital branding, talent representation and content marketing. Sonu has worked extensively with film production houses, OTT platforms and brands to enhance their digital presence and audience engagement. His vision for MC was simple: build a creative home where artists are treated as partners, not products. He is also the creator behind original comic book IP developed under the MC banner.

NJay

Co-founder · Business manager

NJay (Navathanjay Yamjala) brings a strong foundation in account management and digital marketing, with years of experience shaping brand communication and audience engagement across digital channels. Skilled in strategic planning, social media marketing, online advertising and content development, he has played key roles managing client relationships and delivering results-driven campaigns.

Sai Charan

Co-founder · Content specialist

A content strategist and digital marketing expert, Sai Charan has managed high-performance social media assets and led marketing campaigns for films like Sheeshmahal. His work with Avanti Cinema and other media ventures highlights his ability to create impactful digital narratives that connect with audiences on a deep, cultural level.

Our journey

Timeline

2018

MC founded

Started with social media management and an early partnership with Avanti Cinema following the success of Nirudyoga Natulu.

2019

Music distribution begins

First official music distribution deals, taking Avanti Cinema soundtracks to Spotify, Apple Music and more.

2020

Lockdown innovation

Transformed Avanti Cinema's operations during COVID, pioneering new digital screening and community-release models.

2021

Talent roster expands

Signed Jagadeesh Prathap Bandari, Raj Tirandasu and several rising artists. Distributed the Pickpocket OST by Smaran.

2024

Big screen presence

Back-to-back theatrical releases with Avanti Cinema: Double Engine, Sheeshmahal and Chivaraku Migiledhi.

2026

MC Studios

Setting up an in-house content studio and expanding into original IP: audio series, web shows and animated content.
