More than an agency. We are storytellers.

People lived on farms. People lived in cities. Now people live on the Internet. Our job is to present your brand pleasantly.

Chat on WhatsApp

MC was founded in 2018 with one conviction: great stories deserve great distribution.

Before 2018, our founding team spent years building the digital side of Telugu cinema: social platforms, YouTube channels, IMDb and Wikipedia pages and event campaigns for studios including Mythri Movie Makers, Sathya Sai Entertainments and SVC Films.

Then we saw talented artists, filmmakers and creators struggling not with their craft, but with reach, strategy and representation. Starting with social media management and small music distribution deals, we grew into a full-service creative agency.

We don't position ourselves as vendors. We are partners, invested in your craft, your career and your community.
