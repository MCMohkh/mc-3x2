Illu

A Telugu drama by Rohit & Sasi, streaming on ETV Win. MC helped with the pitch deck and release strategy, and manages the music rights and distribution with Tapeloop Records.

Release StrategyMusic DistributionETV Win2026

About the Project

Illu is a Telugu drama directed by Rohit and Sasi, premiering exclusively on ETV Win on 14 June 2026. Produced by Kishore Marisetti under Manohara Arts, it is a heartfelt story about simplicity, emotion and human relationships, scored by Vivek Sagar.

MC supported Rohit & Sasi with the pitch deck and followed up on the release strategy, and, through our partnership with Vivek Sagar's Tapeloop Records, manages the music rights and distributes the film's music across all platforms.
