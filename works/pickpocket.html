Pickpocket

2022 Written & directed by Divya Bandaru
The Film

Pickpocket is a 2022 Telugu film written and directed by Divya Bandaru, her debut, made as a tribute to Robert Bresson's classic Pickpocket (1959).

Synopsis

Devi is an amateur pickpocket who falls in with a local group of thieves. The film follows his friendship with Bhavani, another small-time thief, as the two drift through the city, smoking cigarettes and picking pockets.

Watch

Full film on YouTube  ·  Official trailer  ·  Avanti Cinema

MC's Role

MC was the marketing partner for Pickpocket, handling digital strategy and distribution. The work sat alongside our long-running partnership with Avanti Cinema.

Music

The soundtrack was composed by Smaran (credited as Smaran Sai) and released by Avanti Cinema as an 8-song album, including Thodu Dongalu, Luchagaalu and Devi's Blues.

Soundtrack on Apple Music  ·  Luchagaalu on YouTube  ·  Thodu Dongalu on YouTube

The Next Chapter

Divya Bandaru is working on The Murder Plan (Pickpocket 2), currently in post-production. See her full profile and filmography.
