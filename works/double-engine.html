How it unfolded

The film

Rohit and Sasi's theatrical debut

Double Engine is a Telugu feature film from Avanti Cinema, directed by Rohit Penumatsa and Camp Sasi, the duo behind Nirudyoga Natulu, Kotha Poradu and Story Discussion. It released on 5 January 2024 and marked their first theatrical release.

Our role

Marketing, music, talent and press

As the long-running partner of Avanti Cinema, MC handled digital marketing, music distribution, talent coordination and press strategy for the release.

What followed

A year of releases

Double Engine opened a run of releases from the same unit: Sheeshmahal in February 2024, Chivaraku Migiledhi in December 2024 and Gopi Galla Goa Trip in November 2025. MC supported the campaigns for all of them.
