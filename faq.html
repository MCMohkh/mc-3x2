Frequently AskedQuestions

Quick answers about distribution, representation, hiring talent and our services.

About MC Joint

What is MC Joint?

MC Joint is a creative agency based in Hyderabad, founded in 2018. We handle music distribution, talent management, content production, digital strategy and brand partnerships, branding and design, and digital footprint services such as IMDb and Wikipedia consulting.

Who do you work with?

We work with businesses such as Avanti Cinema, Arha Media, First Show, Tapeloop Records and The Style Mill, with associates we collaborate with from time to time, and with individual talent including composers, actors, lyricists, singers, rappers and filmmakers.

How quickly will you respond?

We read every message personally and usually get back to you within 3–5 business days.

Music distribution

How do I get my music distributed?

Send your links and a few details through the Submit Music / Get Represented form. We distribute to Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube Music, JioSaavn and other major platforms, and help with release strategy, platform pitching, catalogue management and royalty tracking.

What should I include when I submit my music?

Links to your released or unreleased tracks (a private link is fine), what you do, your Instagram or other profiles, and what you need help with. The more context you share, the faster we can reply.

Do you distribute film soundtracks?

Yes. We are the distribution partner for the Avanti Cinema catalogue, including the soundtracks for Chivaraku Migiledhi, Sheeshmahal and Pickpocket, and for Tapeloop Records.

Talent and representation

How do I get represented by MC Joint?

Use the Submit Music / Get Represented form and choose Get represented. Share links to your work and tell us where you are in your career. We review every submission and reply with next steps.

Who does MC Joint represent?

Our roster includes music composers, singers, lyricists, rappers, actors and filmmakers. The full list is on the Talent page.

How can I contact or hire an artist you represent?

Use the Contact a Talent form, or the Get in touch button on any artist's profile. Tell us what you need, whether it's hiring, a collaboration, media, casting, a brand partnership or an event appearance. Our team coordinates with the artist and replies to you.

Services

What are digital footprint services?

We help you be findable, credible and correctly represented online: IMDb management (profile creation, credits and upkeep), Wikipedia consulting (notability and sourcing guidance), Google Knowledge Panel (claim, verify and update) and PR that supports your profile.

Do you offer branding and design?

Yes. We cover brand strategy and research, visual identity and brand guidelines, naming and messaging, digital and web design, print and packaging, environmental graphics and signage, campaigns and key art, and brand rollout.

Can you market my film?

Yes. We plan digital marketing and release campaigns, distribute film music, coordinate talent and press, and build OTT and pay-as-you-like release models. See our Avanti Cinema and Pickpocket case studies.

How much do your services cost?

It depends on the scope of the work. Tell us what you need through the contact form and we will come back with a scope and a timeline.

Still have a question?

Contact us, submit your music or apply for representation, or contact a talent.
