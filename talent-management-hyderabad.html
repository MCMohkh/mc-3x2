Hyderabad

Talent Management
in Hyderabad

We manage careers, not just gigs. MC represents artists, musicians, actors and filmmakers from Hyderabad and across South Indian cinema and music.

MC is a creative agency based in Hyderabad, founded in 2018. We saw talented artists, filmmakers and creators struggling not with their craft but with reach, strategy and representation, and built an agency around closing that gap.

What representation includes

  • Audition and meeting management
  • Brand endorsements and event appearances
  • Press relations and digital PR
  • Liaison between studios, brands and production houses
  • OTT platform pitching and content representation
  • Social media and digital footprint management, including IMDb and Google Knowledge Panel

Who we represent

Our roster spans music composers, singers, lyricists and rappers, and film and web-series actors and filmmakers. Among them are composers Vivek Sagar and Smaran, actors Jagadeesh Prathap Bandari and Pawon Ramesh, and rap artists from the B12 Collective. See the full roster.

Who this is for

  • Artists and actors who want a team handling opportunities, press and positioning. Apply to be represented.
  • Producers, studios and brands looking to hire, cast or collaborate. Contact a talent and we coordinate with the artist.

Proof, not promises

Our longest partnership is with Avanti Cinema, which began in 2018 and now spans digital strategy, OTT development, music distribution and film campaigns.

How we work

  1. Discovery
    We listen. Your goals, your work, your audience and your timeline come first.
  2. Strategy
    A tailored plan: distribution strategy, talent roadmap, content calendar or brand deck.
  3. Execution
    Our team delivers on time, on brand, with clear communication throughout.
  4. Growth
    We measure results, iterate and keep pushing.
