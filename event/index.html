What we run

The operations that decide whether an event feels effortless or chaotic.

  • Ticketing and online bookingsSales, passes and guest lists handled end to end.
  • Entry management and QR scanningFast, accurate entry on the day.
  • On-ground arrangements and logisticsEverything that has to be in place before doors open.
  • Resource, vendor and crew coordinationOne point of contact for the people and kit that make the day work.
  • Show-day operations and artist hospitalityRun-of-show support and a smooth day for performers and guests.
