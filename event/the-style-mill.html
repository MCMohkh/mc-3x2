The Style Mill

Taking a menswear property from two cities to five, with strategy, storytelling and execution.

Start a conversation
ClientThe Style Mill (Yashita and Ridhima)
SeasonSeason 6, Diwali Festive Edit 2026
PeriodJune to October 2026
RoleStrategic partner: creative strategy, campaign direction, content development, digital and event coordination

One brand, five cities: Hyderabad, Surat, Chennai, Jaipur and Raipur.

When we began working with The Style Mill for its Diwali Festive Edit 2026, the assignment was to shape a campaign that could carry the brand beyond its Hyderabad roots and across a five-city tour.

We approached it as more than a series of exhibition promotions. We wanted to help The Style Mill say what it stands for, why it exists and why it matters to the modern Indian man.

How it unfolded

Referral

Introduced by a founder who trusted us

Yashita and Ridhima had hosted The Style Mill in Hyderabad and Chennai for five seasons. For Season 6 they wanted to take it to five cities, and Aishwarya Agarwal of Design District introduced us to oversee operations and creative direction.

The narrative

A dedicated menswear platform with people at the centre

We positioned The Style Mill as a dedicated menswear destination, with a story rooted in the founders' journey, the gap they saw in the market and how men shop. The aim was to move beyond announcing dates, designers and venues and explain why a menswear platform matters, what shoppers can discover and why designers should want to be there.

Founder storytelling became central: their origin story, their contrasting personalities and the thinking behind the business.

The creative

The Monumental Edit

For Season 6 we developed a creative direction called the Monumental Edit, connecting festive and celebration menswear with the heritage and architecture of the five cities. Shoot planning covered founder-led announcements, designer discovery, festive styling, designer conversations, coordinated looks, monument-inspired transitions and event countdowns, along with props, garments, backdrops, lighting and set elements.

Content was built around people, not just products: founder conversations, designer stories, hero pieces, styling advice and city-specific formats. For local collaborators we looked for ways to make them trusted style voices rather than promotional faces. In Raipur we planned immersive event-day storytelling of founders with designers and shoppers discussing what they were looking for.

Execution

Connecting strategy to the systems behind a tour

The scope included website planning, city pages, designer listings, event information, city-wise designer files, stall information, commercial tracking and graphic and PDF assets, tied to social media coordination, content briefs, approvals and publishing. We also supported operational planning, from stall needs and equipment terminology to venue rules. At Hilton Jaipur, the venue's rules on layout, branding, safety, electrical work, setup and dismantling shaped how the event had to run.

The Season 6 microsite, designer directory and check-in tools are live at /thestylemill/.

Five cities

One brand, five local conversations

Hyderabad reinforced the brand's existing identity. Surat required planning and a handover-based arrangement around our physical availability. Chennai and Jaipur called for city-specific campaign coordination, and Raipur opened the door to local creator concepts, founder-led invitations, designer outreach and live content.

The numbers

Reading campaign data honestly

From the exported Meta data for 1 July to 14 October 2026, the MC and The Style Mill campaign group recorded 8.79 million impressions, 26,625 Instagram profile visits and 32,363 link clicks.

These are indicators of activity. They don't establish verified registrations, unique people reached across all campaigns, attendance attribution or designer sales, and we report awareness, engagement, intent and business outcomes separately rather than treating them as interchangeable.
