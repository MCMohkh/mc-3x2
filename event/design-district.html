Design District

A curated fashion exhibition founded by Aishwarya Agarwal, and the systems we helped build behind it.

Start a conversation
ClientDesign District, founded by Aishwarya Agarwal
Our seasonsSeason 2 and Season 3 (1 October 2026)
RoleDigital presence, event operations, QR passes and scanning, designer coordination

From a 60-designer show to 112 designers, in two seasons.

Design District brings independent Indian designers together with an audience that wants to discover them. Our job was never to change that vision. It was to help the founder build the systems, the digital layer and the operations that let it grow without losing its curated character.

  • 112designers in Season 3, up from 60
  • 22,000sq ft hall, up from 5,000
  • 500+QR passes generated in Season 2
  • 1,500+VIP registrations for Season 3
  • 150+people on the team by event day

How it unfolded

September 2025

How Design District began

Design District began in September 2025, when Aishwarya Agarwal brought independent Indian designers together in a curated fashion and lifestyle exhibition of her own. From the start it was built around discovery rather than volume: a space where fashion, couture, jewellery and emerging labels could meet an audience looking for something distinctive.

She chose the designers herself, and that curation became the signature of the show.

Season 2

Where our journey began

We joined Design District at Season 2. We built its landing page, and Aishwarya knew exactly what she wanted: how the page should look, which parts of the brand to represent, and her fonts, colour palette and branding elements. It was a real pleasure to work with a founder who knows her brand that well, and the page was built and published in a short span.

We then supported the show itself. We took on the requirements for the event day, collected the details we needed from designers, and worked in tandem with fabricator Sayed Ilyas Rizvi and the vendors to keep operations smooth. For most of it, Aishwarya made the calls on what stays and what goes, and we helped fulfil what she envisioned.

We also built a QR pass generator and scanner so registration would be smooth and hassle-free. We hit a few bugs on the event day and sorted them out almost immediately, and people loved the digital registration. Season 2 generated over 500 passes for a one-day event, with about 60 designers in 5,000 sq ft at Park Hyatt.

Season 3

Bigger, and just as hand-picked

Preparation for Season 3 began just five days after Season 2 ended: finalising the venue, vendors, fabrication and decor, and a very long run of calls and follow-ups. We took a larger role, but it was Aishwarya's show throughout. She decides what goes where, when and how, and stands there in person to make sure it does.

The show moved to Hall 4 at HICC, Novotel on 1 October 2026. The designer line-up grew from 60 to 112, and the hall from 5,000 sq ft to 22,000 sq ft. Even now, Aishwarya personally finds each designer, follows them and goes through their social feed, for every designer in every show.

The decor was in the hands of Mr. Minttu Sarna, a celebrated Hyderabad decor designer, and his entire team, who worked with us throughout. Sayed Ilyas Rizvi handled the stalls and fabrication. The scale of this season owes as much to them as to anyone.

The press line

Newspapers will come to us

Many events would have bought newspaper ads. We told Aishwarya we'd make this season so big that the newspapers would come to us.

About a week after the event, the Times of India ran a piece about Design District in Hyderabad Spotlight. None of us knew it was coming. Their team had visited the event, taken pictures and used others from the website, and finding the article was a complete surprise.

Three days before the show, Aishwarya was also awarded Hyderabad's Outstanding Curated Fashion Exhibition at the Times Business Awards Hyderabad 2026, 12th Edition.

Event day

Traffic on the road to Novotel

So many people showed up that the road leading to Novotel was clogged. Unlike earlier events, the first two hours saw a third of the attendance. We left no stone unturned on marketing, PR, invites and VIP passes (powered by MC), and skipped WhatsApp marketing this season to keep the event niche.

We received more than 1,500 VIP registrations for the VIP hours of 10:30 to 12:30. As usual with a surge nobody predicts, our system failed under the load on the registration link and again on the event day with heavy walk-ins and scans. Like Aishwarya would, we pushed through and brought the system back online in 22 minutes.

What we built and ran

Digital tools and operations around the event, always in service of the founder's vision.

  • Landing page and website
  • Interactive Hall 4 floor map with search and category filters
  • QR pass generator and entry scanning
  • VIP registration and RSVP
  • Stall setup and invoice-request page
  • Designer communication and accessories lists
  • SEO foundations and sharing previews
  • Event-day requirements, fabricators, vendors and decor coordination
  • Media outreach and invitations

It takes a village

Aishwarya makes sure her event runs smoothly for every designer, stall, vendor and team member. Her core team was about eight people, and by event day the team working for her was more than 150, including our decor and fabrication partners, who worked as full teams.
